Who We Are

GitGO-QSoft and Research LLP, founded in 2019 in Ghaziabad, India, is the parent company of GitGO Technologies. GitGO Technologies delivers innovative IT solutions including software development, web & mobile applications, and IT consulting. We use AI, Cloud, and Data Analytics to drive digital transformation and create scalable, robust systems. Agile methodologies and iterative processes ensure efficiency, quality, and client satisfaction. Our core values are innovation, transparency, and delivering measurable results in every project.
